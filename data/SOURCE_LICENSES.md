# Local demonstration source provenance

The MVP includes three brief excerpts from the NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook. Each record in corpus.json has a fixed URL, section locator, source ID, review date and exact excerpt; […] marks omitted text. D checked the excerpts against the official pages on October 24, 2026. They support general experimental-design reasoning and do not establish chemical safety requirements, course compliance or approval to conduct an experiment. Instructor review remains required before classroom use.
